Pick the right plan.

Cost · $0 to $100/mo Needed · honesty about how you'll use it Difficulty · gentle

Here's the honest pitch, up front: if you're going to use Claude regularly, get the $100/month Max plan. The rest of this chapter explains why — and gives you a careful way to step in if you're not sure yet.

The recommendation

Max is the plan I'd put my own money on for anyone who plans to use Claude even a few times a week. It removes the daily message ceiling, gives you the strongest models, and lets you use Claude Code in your terminal without watching a meter. No friction, no rationing your good ideas.

The math

Think of Max like a gym membership — better, think of it like an index fund for your own capability. If Claude saves you one hour a week and you value your time at $30/hr, the plan pays for itself within the month. Most people who get into the daily rhythm save substantially more.

This isn't a lifestyle metaphor. It's a capability purchase. You're buying access to a patient collaborator who will work alongside you on writing, thinking, planning, coding, and learning — for less than a single hour with a tutor or consultant. The cost of not having Claude available when you need it is real, and it compounds.

An honest caveat Don't pay for what you won't use. If you spend a month subscribed and barely log in, cancel. The advice is "invest in yourself," not "set $100 on fire."

Not sure yet? A stepped path

  1. Week one: stay on Free. Try Claude on a few real problems. Learn the texture.
  2. Hitting limits and frustrated: upgrade to Pro ($20/mo). Many people stay here happily forever.
  3. Using Claude Code daily and the Pro ceiling starts to feel like a leash: step up to Max. You'll know when.

If you already know — if you're a working professional, a serious learner, or you've read this far thinking "yes, finally, sign me up" — skip the runway and start on Max. Your time is the asset you're optimizing for.

The full menu

  • Free — $0. Daily message limit. A capable model. Perfect for tasting.
  • Pro — $20/month. Higher limits, strongest models in the browser, access to Claude Code. The sweet spot for casual-to-frequent use.
  • Max — $100/month and up. The biggest usage budget, no need to think about limits, ideal for daily Claude Code users. The plan this guide quietly assumes you'll end up on.

How to subscribe

On claude.ai, click your name in the bottom-left corner, then Settings → Plans. Pick a plan, enter a card, and you're done. You can cancel from the same screen any time.

Try this now Open claude.ai. Click your name. Find the Plans page. You don't have to subscribe today — just see where it lives, so the choice feels less abstract when you're ready.
One last note: the site you're reading is free and always will be. The only thing that ever costs money in this guide is your relationship with Anthropic — and that's a choice you make on your own timeline.
