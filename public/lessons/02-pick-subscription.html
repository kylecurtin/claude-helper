Pick the right plan.

Cost · $0 to start Needed · honesty about how you'll use it Difficulty · gentle

Anthropic offers a few different plans. They all give you Claude — the difference is how much you can talk to Claude per day and which models you can pick. Here's a plain-language guide.

The free plan

Yes, there's a free plan. You get a daily budget of messages, you can chat about anything, and you can use a capable Claude model. The free plan is enough to:

  • Try Claude on real questions and see if it earns a place in your day
  • Read through this entire guide and try the examples
  • Get a feel for the pace of conversation

Start here. Almost everyone should.

Pro

Pro is about $20 a month. You get a much larger usage budget, access to Claude's strongest models, and — importantly for this guide — access to Claude Code, the terminal tool we install in Chapter 03. If you're going to use Claude most days, especially for coding or longer writing, Pro is the sweet spot.

How to know it's time If you've hit the free plan's daily limit twice in a week and felt frustrated, that's your sign. Until then, save your money.

Max

Max is for power users — typically developers using Claude Code many hours a day. It costs more (starting around $100/month) and gives you the biggest usage budget. Most readers of this guide won't need it. If you do, you'll know.

So what do I pick?

Honest advice, for almost everyone reading this:

  1. Today: stay on Free. Use it for a week.
  2. If you love it and hit limits: upgrade to Pro. The annual option saves a bit if you're sure.
  3. If you start using Claude Code seriously every day: Max might pay for itself in time saved.

How to upgrade, when the time comes

On claude.ai, click your name in the bottom-left corner, then Settings → Plans. Pick the plan you want, enter a card, and you're done. You can cancel from the same screen if you change your mind.

A note on this guide: The site you're reading is and will remain free. The only thing that ever costs money is your relationship with Anthropic — and even that is optional for a long while.
